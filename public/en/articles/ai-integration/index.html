Connect AI to JLPT Master

Let your AI assistant work with your actual study records, then check its saved results in JLPT Master. You can read this guide without signing in; personal data requires authorization.

One goal for this article

Add the MCP connection, authorize it, and verify one read-only request.

A concrete example

Illustrative example, not your study record

You want an AI assistant to help with vocabulary you have already saved. First, ask it to list your wordbooks without changing anything.

What a good result looks like

Illustrative result; verify against real data

A successful check returns your real wordbook names, or an explicitly empty list, with no tool error. A generic answer about vocabulary is not proof of access.

A prompt you can use

Use JLPT Master to list my wordbooks and their names. Do not create or change any data. If the list is empty or the connection fails, tell me exactly what happened.

Three practical steps

  1. Add https://jlpt.erzhiqian.cc/api/jlpt/mcp as a remote MCP server in a client that supports custom MCP connections. A localhost URL will not work for a cloud client.

  2. Sign in on the authorization page and review the requested access. Reading, writing, and audio access have different permissions.

  3. Run the read-only request above. Then open your wordbooks in JLPT Master and compare the result before asking AI to save anything.

Where to check the result

Open your wordbooks and confirm the names match. After you later save an item, check that it appears in the intended book; a chat reply alone is not a saved result.

What to watch for

Client support for custom MCP and audio varies. You can review or revoke connected AI access in your account. Check generated readings, examples, answers, and explanations before using them.
