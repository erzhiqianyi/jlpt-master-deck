Keep an unknown word with its reading context

A word can be easy to misremember once removed from a passage. Save the sentence and source first, then decide how to review it.

One goal for this article

Keep the sentence and reading source before explaining the word.

A concrete example

Illustrative example, not your study record

来年度は利用者の増加を見込んでいる。 What does 見込む express in this sentence?

What a good result looks like

Illustrative result; verify against real data

Here 見込む means “expect” or “forecast,” referring to a rise in users next year. Keep this sentence and the article title, then add another example with a different collocation.

A prompt you can use

Read my marked reading capture in JLPT Master Deck. Quote its sentence and article title, explain 見込む in that context, give its reading and two examples, and check for an existing entry. Save it only if the source is clear. Do not infer the whole article from one sentence.

Three practical steps

  1. Mark the word in the reading page and save the surrounding sentence.

  2. Check the capture, source, and word boundary before organizing it.

  3. Find the resulting vocabulary entry and mark the capture processed only after saving succeeds.

Where to check the result

Check the capture status and search for 見込む in your wordbook. Its meaning should match the saved sentence.

What to watch for

One sentence supports a local interpretation, not claims about the entire article or its source.
