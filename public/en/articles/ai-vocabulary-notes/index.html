What a good result looks like

Illustrative result; verify against real data

Record 見落とす（みおとす）, “to overlook or miss something that should have been noticed,” and the original sentence. Compare it with 見逃す using contextual examples rather than claiming they are always interchangeable.

A prompt you can use

Check my JLPT Master vocabulary for 見落とす. If it exists, update the same entry. Otherwise save its reading, meaning, part of speech, original sentence, two natural examples, and a contextual comparison with 見逃す in my N1 wordbook. Report the actual saved location.

Three practical steps

  1. Keep the complete source sentence in your capture or request.

  2. Search your existing items before creating another entry.

  3. Check the reading and examples, save the entry, and find it in the target wordbook.

Where to check the result

Search the target wordbook for 見落とす and check its reading, source sentence, meaning, and comparison.

What to watch for

The two verbs overlap in some contexts. Treat any simple distinction as a study note to check against real usage.
